## The brief

Atlas began with a simple question: how might planning software feel less like administration and more like a creative tool? The work focused on reducing visual noise while keeping complex projects easy to understand.

## The approach

I mapped the team’s working rhythm, prototyped the highest-friction moments, and built a modular visual system that could grow with the product. Clear hierarchy, expressive color, and purposeful motion help people stay oriented without getting in the way.

## The brief

Field Notes needed a visual language with the character of an independent journal and the clarity of a contemporary archive. It had to support long-form writing, photography, and short observations without feeling like a template.

## The approach

The identity pairs a warm editorial palette with a precise typographic grid. Modular story blocks allow each feature to find its own rhythm while shared details keep the publication recognizable.
